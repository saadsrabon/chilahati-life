# Station and character correction

The first generic station and rigid figures were rejected by the user. This correction replaces them with a more specific architectural interpretation and articulated people.

## Chilahati Railway Station

Reference photographs show the contemporary station with a repeated folded or sawtooth concrete canopy, red-brick facade, slender silver columns, broad entrance, bilingual station name, patterned forecourt and striped low rails.

- Photograph by shamimhossain: https://steematlas.com/@shamimhossain/chilahati-railway-station-chilahati
- Front reference: https://cdn.steemitimages.com/DQmUpHhFRvFcETMnQCipPDoBt2RpAywD7yVEuT3pvvjaBqQ/1000055410.jpg
- Side reference: https://cdn.steemitimages.com/DQmUTBXJN65sfiY8CHzvDv3ep51aDPWFqi8NUmRoV43m8qF/1000055402.jpg
- Inauguration reporting and photograph dated 4 November 2023: https://www.dhakapost.com/country/235282

These photographs inform manually modeled geometry. They are not bundled as game textures. Building dimensions, exact placement and surrounding streets remain illustrative; the entire station has not been surveyed.

## People and movement

Figures use more human proportions, hip and shoulder pivots, bending knees, alternating arm swing, smooth starts and turns, and seated vehicle poses. Clothing includes checked lungi, shirts, panjabi with pajama, and salwar kameez with orna. These remain procedural stylized people, not scanned or photorealistic humans.

The corrective build passes TypeScript and production compilation. Its new appearance and movement have not been visually re-tested because Computer Use stopped when it could not verify the browser URL. Android visual verification also remains pending.
